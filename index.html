Effort

RPE = how hard a set was, out of 10. Judge it on the last rep.

6–73–4 more reps possible. Bar moves quickly, feels easy. Deload and fillers. 82 more. Last rep slows a little, form stays clean. 91 more. Last rep is a clear grind. You'd bet on one more, not two. 10No more. The next rep would fail or break form. Isolation last sets only.

Isolation = lateral raise, curls, triceps, leg curls. Squat and deadlift never above RPE 9 (deadlift top set RPE 8).

Tempo: lower every rep under control, about 2 s.

Bad day: top set 2+ reps short of last time → skip it, do back-offs only.
